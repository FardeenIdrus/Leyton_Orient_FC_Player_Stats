How similarity is computed
  1. Who Rated players at the same position and season.
  2. Each measure against his own league How far above or below his league's average, in standard deviations. Example: 0.40 xG per 90 where the league average is 0.25 (sd 0.10) gives +1.5.
  3. Gap His figure minus the other player's. Example: +1.5 vs +0.5 gives a gap of 1.0.
  4. Weight Each gap counts by the measure's share of the Overall rating.
  5. Linked measures Measures that move together (e.g. Sprints and Sprint distance) count once, not twice.
  6. Distance All gaps combined into one number: the smaller, the more alike.
  7. Similarity % Take every possible pair of wingers this season (any two, all leagues). 85% means these two are more alike than 85% of those pairs.
