### ⚠️ THE `.env` TRAP — cost a full round of wrong conclusions, do not repeat

`docker compose` injects `env_file:` values at CONTAINER CREATION. Editing `.env` then
`docker compose restart` does **NOT** update them — the old value survives as an environment
variable, and pydantic-settings gives environment variables precedence over the file.

The platform ran against a COPY of the scouting sheet for two days while `.env` said
otherwise, and because the copy was only 12 reports behind, the matching counts read as
confirmation.

**Use `docker compose up -d --force-recreate app dashboard`, then verify:**
`docker compose exec -T app printenv SCOUT_SHEET_ID`

### Two further deployment facts learned the hard way

- `--force-recreate` recreates a container from the **existing image**. It does not rebuild.
  After a dependency change you need `deploy.sh` (or `--build`).
- `deploy.sh` ships code and runs migrations. It does **not** re-run scoring. Anything that
  changes stored scores needs `python -m lofc.model.scorecard_run` afterwards.
- `secrets/` is gitignored and never travels with a deploy. The key must be copied by hand
  and owned by UID 1000 (the container user), or the app cannot read it.

### RESOLVED — "A New Player" name recovery (2026-09-30)

**20 of 449 live reports** carried the dropdown's escape-hatch value. The scout writes the
real name into the Report Comments; the Head of Recruitment later adds the player to the
dropdown by hand, but the filed report keeps saying "A New Player". Confirmed current, not
historical — the 20 span 18 Aug to 26 Sep, and the sheet's `Database` tab is byte-identical
to `Form responses` and corrects none of them.

`name_from_comment()` reads the name from the first line, handling the four observed shapes
(name + full stop; name + verb; name + shirt number in brackets; leading shirt number +
name + dash). **20 of 20 recovered, 0 left as placeholder, 0 wrongly made linkable.**

**A recovered name IS matched to our players, on a second tier.** The form's dropdown and
our `players` table are different lists: 9 of the 20 are players we hold full data on,
mostly Premier League 2, whose reports previously opened nothing. They cannot use the strict
name + date-of-birth rule — there is no date, which is why they were missing from the
dropdown — so they match on NAME ALONE, with safety coming from refusing ambiguity: a name
held by two of our players links to neither. Live result: **9 matched, 0 ambiguous.** This
tier is reached ONLY by a recovered name; a dropdown-named player has a date available and
is never downgraded to name-only matching.

Rules pinned by tests (`tests/test_scout_new_player_name.py` 27, `test_scout_report_link.py` 21):
- A comment that does not open with something name-shaped keeps the placeholder.
- A candidate equal to the club name is refused (reports often open by setting the scene).

**Self-healing and automatic.** Extraction is a step inside the ordinary 60-second sync, not
a separate script. It fires only while the Player field still reads "A New Player", so a
corrected row in the sheet takes over on the next sync with no migration or cleanup.

Marked on screen with `†` and a column tooltip. Column `scout_reports.name_from_report`,
migration `26e5e1b5fff3`.

## B. SCORING FORMULA REVIEW

### Three defects found and fixed. All live. None changed the ranking except the first.

**1. Percentile scale was off-centre — fixed 2026-09-30.**
`metric_percentiles` used `rank(pct=True)` = rank ÷ n, so percentiles averaged 50 + 50/n,
never 50. Small pools were inflated most: pools under 30 players averaged **3.14** against
**3.01** for pools of 100+, correlation −0.725 with pool size. Fixed to `(rank − 0.5) ÷ n`.
Verified after re-scoring: Performance averages exactly 3.00 in 55 of 56 groups, Physical in
all 42 that have it. 25/26 mean 3.0294 → **2.9972**; **660 of 3,345 ratings changed rank**,
max 4 places.

*A second defect was introduced and caught during that fix* — dividing by pool size instead
of the count of players holding the metric, which would have deflated every metric with
missing values to a band of 1.0.

**2. Archetype drops did nothing — fixed 2026-10-02.**
`performance_metrics_for_archetype` dropped by the CLUB's metric name. Two club entries can
resolve to the same stored metric, so dropping one left the twin and the measurement
survived. **Winger / "Crossing & Creative Threat" dropped 0 of 1 and scored all 415 wingers
identically to the full list** — offered in the interface, completely inert. Full Back /
"Attacking" achieved 2 of 3. Now matched on the resolved metric.
**`objective_composite` moved for zero players** — only the two broken archetype views changed.

**3. The report itself carried errors.** Found while the Head of Recruitment reviewed it;
see "Report status" below.
